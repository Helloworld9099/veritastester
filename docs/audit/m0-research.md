## Evidence and release gaps

- The three publisher-pinned Marian model pages mark **Apache 2.0**; [Amazon MASSIVE official notice](https://github.com/alexa/massive/blob/main/NOTICE.md) marks corpus **CC BY 4.0**. Preserve notices; reconcile [Tatoeba sentence/user attribution](https://tatoeba.org/en/terms_of_use) and [ManyThings source snapshot / license provenance](https://www.manythings.org/anki/), plus upstream OPUS provenance and release packaging. For FR Stage5, the [Global Voices policy](https://globalvoices.org/about/global-voices-attribution-policy/) is attribution-permissive by default **unless otherwise stated**, not proof of each article pair’s clearance; retain original URLs/authors/translators, specific licenses/exceptions and required notices. [WMT24 Chat author terms](https://wmt-chat-task.github.io/2024/) are CC BY-NC 4.0, and the repository marks the historical ES subtitles NC. No commercial activation or license-clearance claim.
- [Actual multilingual run](https://github.com/Helloworld9099/veritastester/actions/runs/44821438496) completed successfully: original BridgeCaption text Pipeline used for real ES→EN, FR→EN, EN→ES inference with pinned model/adapters and ES/FR rollback inference; bounded one-model cache; archived 20/20 ES→EN and EN→ES **DEV** outputs token/string parity, peak 1,057,180 KiB process RSS. French single-route loading/translation/rollback ≠ French comparable output parity or independent adequacy. The separate earlier unit suite reports 29 tests. This is not cross-platform mic→ASR→caption, streaming/chunking, or sustained-latency proof.
- The 500-row packet is DEV by definition. Its mapped notes flag 178 possible rows (72 ES→EN, 106 EN→ES); 322 unmentioned are unknown, no formal assessable pass/fail labels. [Three-loop ablation](https://github.com/Helloworld9099/veritastester/actions/runs/44821644158), [decoder comparison](https://github.com/Helloworld9099/veritastester/actions/runs/44821981465), and [sentence segmentation](https://github.com/Helloworld9099/veritastester/actions/runs/44822351772) executed real ES inference, but cleaner/nonlooping output still omitted or altered meaning and was not promoted. Plain exact parity on reviewed examples is not independent testing. Shared adapters both reject past-context input and >128-token input instead of silently concatenating/truncating; that avoids earlier leakage but is not context-aware decoding.
- No independently bilingual-rated 95% for **any** direction; no adjudicated, fresh sealed 2,000-per-direction holdout verified; no formal held-out slang/regional/pronoun/number/ordinal/omission equivalence. Existing FLORES diagnostic, user-reviewed 500, synthetic stress, repeatedly selected DEV and historical tests should not be called fresh holdouts. Direction-level evaluation, known register/context shortcomings, quality and CPU/RSS comparison to incumbent, compatible rollback and license/device QA are release gates. Preserve independent directions and the incumbent; do not activate adapters or merge incompatible tokenizers/weights for M0.
