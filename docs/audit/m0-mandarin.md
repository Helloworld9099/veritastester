# BridgeCaption Mandarin→English: M0 checkpoint audit

**Status: checkpoint lineage verified; deterministic development baseline not run.** This is a private, noncommercial research direction, not an active application translator. No training, held-out evaluation, runtime integration, weights publication, or paid compute was performed for M0. Nothing here establishes translation quality or a 95% adequacy rate.

- **Base:** [IndexTeam/Index-Translate-2B](https://huggingface.co/IndexTeam/Index-Translate-2B) at immutable revision `9bc08445315723eb9b77d1b93505915eb03c3f01`. The pinned publisher card says Apache-2.0. The repository revision and upstream weight metadata were verified without fetching the 4.55 GB weight file; the tokenizer bytes were fetched and checksum-verified. No model inference was attempted.
- **Latest adapter:** A separate rank-8 LoRA checkpoint from a completed 20-update research stage. Forty newly authored synthetic Mandarin utterances were each presented with and without irrelevant preceding text, with the same English target. This stage was designed to teach the model to ignore irrelevant history; completion does not show that it learned this behavior.
- **Preservation:** Eight private archives and all 32 checkpoint members listed in their checksums were verified, with seven consecutive adapter links and two final optimizer/RNG links. Major recovered stages include MELI interview speech, AISHELL-4 arranged discussion, original RiSAWOZ written roleplay, CS-Dialogue conversational speech, matched original RiSAWOZ conditions, and two synthetic paired conditions. These are different source types. AI-authored and independently rechecked English is not human gold.
- **Rights:** The Apache-2.0 base does not clear the adapter or its dataset ancestry. Apply the [original RiSAWOZ publisher’s CC BY-NC 4.0 terms](https://github.com/terryqj0107/RiSAWOZ), not a more permissive mirror label; the recovered MELI, AISHELL-4 and CS-Dialogue experiments were also scoped for noncommercial research. Keep weights, optimizer state, training rows, and generated outputs out of public artifacts and shipping routes pending a full rights review.
- **Integration boundary:** Mandarin is not a registry route in [PR #43](https://github.com/Helloworld9099/veritastester/pull/43). It uses a different Qwen3.5-based architecture and tokenizer from the Marian Spanish and French models. Do not blend their weights, treat this audit as a release gate pass, or substitute a quantized/base-only result for the exact private adapter.
- **Future private development baseline:** Technically feasible with the pinned weights already cached, the original offline CPU software environment, and enough memory. Use a handful of newly authored non-sensitive Mandarin inputs; compare current-only and coherent synthetic prior conditions; run twice from fresh processes with fixed decode settings. Capture byte-level replay, measured latency and peak process RSS. Keep the baseline private and leave prior benchmark inputs and gold sealed. M0 did not measure runtime, determinism, translation quality or production readiness for Mandarin.
