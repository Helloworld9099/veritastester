# BridgeCaption scientific optimization decisions — 2026-11-21

**Scope:** translate real English/Spanish/French speech with slang, pragmatics and context while expanding the language *architecture*, not falsely announcing that thousands of other languages are adequate. All research decisions below are hypotheses until reproduced on our own bilingual reviewed data.

## What gets measured

Separate from chrF and BLEU, qualified bilingual judges need to label: meaning preserved, correct slang sense, appropriate use of past turns, preservation of negation/modality, numbers/names, speaker roles and gender, register, omissions/hallucinations, interrupted/toddler speech and naturalness. Group train/dev/final by conversation and speaker. High-severity errors must never be hidden by aggregate average or beautiful fluency.

The initial extra Tatoeba+MASSIVE training job measures six routes on **36 newly selected publisher validation cases per route** and preserves the 24-, 48-, and 72-step candidate checkpoints plus the 38-update parent. These examples are development, not final gold. A prespecified gate refuses any >1 chrF or >1 BLEU loss on a direction or worsened high-confidence changed-digit proxy. Selection still remains **research-only** without independent bilingual labels.

## Risks and priorities

1. **Actual competence is blocked by rights and data** for most world languages. Cataloging >8k languages is not equivalent to adding 8k tokenizer code embeddings or finding 8k licensed bilingual corpora.
2. **Data quality > sample count:** previous review caught wrong pronouns, changed time of day, missing clauses, repetitions, literal idioms, register flips. Synthetic paraphrases must have an independently verified translation reference before supervised use.
3. **Context must be past-only:** one tokenizer setting cannot magically teach conversation semantics. Require aligned dialogues and compare condition A (current only), B (past 1–4 turns), C (verified sense definition); hold release if increased latency or more hallucinations.
4. **Preserve earlier winners:** original ES→EN and FR→EN specialist weights remain checksum pinned; newer shared-model candidate may not replace them unless human meaning and latency comparisons show nonregression.
5. **Do not run the final holdout repeatedly.** Model selection uses frozen developer data; qualified reviewers perform independent final checks once after freezing the candidate.

No paid API/compute use authorized. No noncommercial WMT24 Chat, TED2020 CC BY-NC-ND, or unverified OpenSubtitles pairs were ingested.
