# Spanish–English datasets screened for BridgeCaption v2 (2026-11-20)

**Current ingested for training:** Amazon MASSIVE's original published **training split only**, CC BY 4.0, verified via the publisher card. The license-compatible model starts from previously learned MASSIVE Stage 3 weights and not the noncommercial subtitle adapter. **No new 100,000 high-quality examples have been acquired or validated.** Treat the 100,000 target as a candidate budget, not an accomplished milestone.

## Prioritized gap

Acquire and verify usable **genuine** English↔Spanish conversational translations with speaker/context groups and slang/idioms, rather than increasing synthetic examples indiscriminately. Preserve the true provenance of all synthetic data. The private final holdout requires ~2,000 examples **per direction**, a group/speaker-disjoint design and two real bilingual reviewers; no existing public file has been designated and sealed as this new final benchmark.

## Guardrails

- Source terms must be evaluated at the exact dataset release, including upstream contributors and derivative rights; permissive repository metadata alone is not a comprehensive warranty.
- 'We ran a corpus quality script' must never be misreported as 'we verified semantic equivalence.' A publisher-provided reference may still have natural paraphrases and translation errors.
- Never put unlicensed/mislicensed source sentences into a public PR or use them for model training, error mining or synthetic generation.
- Do not request or purchase paid APIs, accelerator instances or proprietary teacher inference under the $0 authorization.
